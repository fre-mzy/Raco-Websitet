Happy Birthday, Mrs. Elizabeth Adesoye Daniel

Honoring our Executive Supervisor & Home Coordinator—a woman of grace, compassion, and divine purpose, and a loving mother to all children at RACO Child Orphanage.

Celebrating Mrs. Elizabeth Adesoye Daniel

Executive Supervisor & Home Coordinator

Today, the entire family of RACO Child and Rural Care Initiative gathers in joy and thanksgiving to celebrate a remarkable woman of faith, resilience, and boundless love: Mrs. Elizabeth Adesoye Daniel.

In her pivotal role as Executive Supervisor and Home Coordinator, her daily touch nurtures every child under our care. Her dedication ensures that our home remains a beacon of hope, restoration, and light across rural communities.

United in Mission

Standing Alongside Our Visionary

Visionary & Founder — RACO Initiative

Together with her husband, the Visionary of RACO Initiative, Mrs. Elizabeth Adesoye Daniel leads with unified faith and unwavering devotion. Their joint vision continues to touch thousands of young lives and bring transformation to rural families.
