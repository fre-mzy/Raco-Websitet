Our Mission

Nurture the Vulnerable. Educate the Next Generation.

Since 2003, RACO Initiative has been dedicated to lifting orphaned children out of poverty, restoring dignity to widows, and delivering direct healthcare to underserved rural communities.

What We Do

Our 6 Core Pillars

01

Orphanage & Shelter

Providing a safe home, nutritious daily meals, emotional support, and parental care to orphaned and vulnerable children.

02

100% Free Primary Education

Operating tuition-free nursery and primary education complete with free uniforms, books, school bags, and daily lunches.

03

Widows Empowerment

Equipping rural widows with vocational training, trade tools, and micro-grants to build independent, sustainable incomes.

04

Rural Health Clinics

Organizing free medical outreaches, health checkups, and prescription distribution for communities lacking basic health access.

05

Back-To-School Sponsorships

Sponsoring school bags, writing materials, instructional media, and settling school bills across primary, secondary, and tertiary levels.

06

Community Development

Building sustainable clean water systems and basic infrastructure improvements across remote rural villages.
