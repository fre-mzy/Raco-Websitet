Faith-Based Care, Protection & Hope

RACO CHILD ORPHANAGE is a care and protection center for orphans, vulnerable, and displaced poor children in rural communities. We are committed to providing hope, dignity, and a future to children in need by meeting their daily needs—meals, shelter, welfare, education, and emotional support.

"Our Mission: To nurture and protect vulnerable children, educate the next generation, and empower rural communities—especially women, youths, and the aged—with tools, training, and opportunities for sustainable development."
